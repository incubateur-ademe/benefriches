# QA 35 — BEN-35: photovoltaic project asks about decontamination in a single step

## Setup and assumptions

The e2e stack (`docker-compose.e2e.yml` + `.env.e2e`, from the repository root) must run images **built from this branch after BEN-35 was implemented**: `make e2e-up-build`. Only the `web` image changes (photovoltaic project wizard, creation and update); no migration, no new environment variable. The guide never restarts or stops a container.

- Web app / API (proxied): `http://localhost:3001`.
- Wizard pages are identified by their heading. The ones this guide relies on:
  - Contract duration: "Quelle sera la durée prévisionnelle du contrat de la revente d'énergie au distributeur ?"
  - "Remise en état": "Le projet prévoit-il une remise en état du site ?"
  - Decontamination introduction: "Et si on dépolluait les sols ?" (text "Le site existant comporte 2 000 m² de sols pollués.")
  - **Merged decontamination step**: "Est-il nécessaire de dépolluer les sols ?" (the old "Est-il **est** nécessaire…" typo is gone)
  - Soils transformation introduction: "Nous allons maintenant parler de ce que seront les sols du site."
  - Reinstatement expenses: "Dépenses de travaux de remise en état de la friche", with a "Dépollution des sols" amount field.
  - Update summary: the "Récapitulatif" of `/mes-projets/<id>/modifier`.
- Before BEN-35 the photovoltaic wizard asked "Est-il est nécessaire de dépolluer les sols ?" (Oui / Non / Ne sait pas), then, for "Oui", a separate page "Quelle part des sols pollués sera dépolluée ?". Its update stepper listed "Remise en état", "Choix de dépolluer" and "Surface à dépolluer" under "Travaux" (the creation stepper only shows the top-level groups, e.g. "Travaux"). The urban wizard was merged by BEN-34 and must keep working (S9).
- Before BEN-35, "Ne sait pas" generated a "Dépollution des sols" reinstatement expense of **0 €** (computed from 0 m²) while the 25 % surface was submitted with the project. S5 is the regression check.
- The merged step's URL query string is `etape=depollution-sols`; the old `etape=selection-depollution-sols` and `etape=surface-depollution-sols` no longer exist.
- The surface input mode (% / ㎡) is a global preference: switching it on one page keeps it on the next surface page.

### Reaching the merged decontamination step of a PV creation

"Reach the merged step" means:

1. Open `http://localhost:3001/creer-projet?siteId=<FRICHE_POLLUTED>`.
2. "A quelle phase du projet êtes-vous ?": pick "Montage / Développement", click "Valider".
3. "Que savez vous de votre projet ?": pick "J'ai des données précises", click "Valider".
4. "Quel type de projet souhaitez-vous évaluer ?": pick "Centrale d'énergie renouvelable", click "Valider".
5. "Quel système d'EnR souhaitez-vous installer ?": pick "Photovoltaïque", click "Valider".
6. "Quel est le paramètre déterminant pour la centrale photovoltaïque ?": pick "La puissance de l'installation", click "Valider".
7. Puissance: `296`, "Valider". Superficie: `2700`, "Valider". Production annuelle: keep the suggested value, "Valider". Contract duration: `20`, "Valider".
8. "Le projet prévoit-il une remise en état du site ?": pick "Oui, le projet inclut des travaux de remise en état", click "Valider".
9. "Et si on dépolluait les sols ?" with "Le site existant comporte 2 000 m² de sols pollués.": click "Suivant".
10. Observe: the heading is "Est-il nécessaire de dépolluer les sols ?".

## S0 — The stack runs this branch

1. Run `docker compose --env-file .env.e2e -f docker-compose.e2e.yml ps` from the repository root. Observe: `web` and `api` are `running` / `healthy`, and their creation time is after the BEN-35 build.

## S1 — The merged step: title, options, stepper and URL

1. Reach the merged step (Setup).
2. Observe: the heading is "Est-il nécessaire de dépolluer les sols ?" (no "est" twice). Three radios: "Oui", "Non", "Ne sait pas" with the hint "Bénéfriches appliquera un ratio de 25% aux sols pollués.". None is selected.
3. Observe: no "Part à dépolluer" input is shown, and the next button reads "Passer" (enabled).
4. Observe the address bar: the query string contains `etape=depollution-sols`.
5. Observe the left stepper: "Travaux" is the current group (the creation stepper shows groups only; its sub-items are checked in update mode, S7). No "Choix de dépolluer" nor "Surface à dépolluer" text anywhere on the page.
6. Click "Précédent". Observe: the heading is "Et si on dépolluait les sols ?". Click "Suivant": back on "Est-il nécessaire de dépolluer les sols ?".

## S2 — "Oui" reveals the surface input, with max validation and % ↔ ㎡ toggle

1. On the merged step (S1), pick "Oui".
2. Observe: right below "Oui" (above "Non"), a "Part à dépolluer" input appears with the "%" / "㎡" toggle ("%" checked) and the hint "Surface contaminée : 2 000 m²". The button now reads "Valider" and is **disabled** (empty input).
3. Enter `150`. Observe: "Valider" stays disabled.
4. Replace with `60`. Observe: the hint under the input reads "💡 Soit 1 200 m²" and "Valider" is enabled.
5. Click "㎡". Observe: the input now shows `1200` and the hint reads "💡 Soit 60%".
6. Replace with `2500`. Observe: "Valider" is disabled. Replace with `1200`: "Valider" is enabled.
7. Pick "Non". Observe: the "Part à dépolluer" input disappears; the button reads "Valider" and is enabled. Pick "Ne sait pas": the input stays hidden.
8. Pick "Oui" again. Observe: the input reappears **empty** and "Valider" is disabled.
9. Click "%" to put the input mode back to percentage for the next scenarios.

## S3 — "Passer" records "Ne sait pas"; navigation to and from the soils transformation introduction

1. Reach the merged step (fresh creation: reload `http://localhost:3001/creer-projet?siteId=<FRICHE_POLLUTED>` and redo the Setup path). Don't select anything; click "Passer".
2. Observe: the heading is "Nous allons maintenant parler de ce que seront les sols du site." — no "Quelle part des sols pollués sera dépolluée ?" page.
3. Click "Précédent". Observe: the heading is "Est-il nécessaire de dépolluer les sols ?" with "Ne sait pas" selected and no surface input.

## S4 — "Non" and "Oui + surface", revisit pre-fill in the current input mode

1. On the merged step (continuing S3), pick "Non", click "Valider".
2. Observe: the heading is "Nous allons maintenant parler de ce que seront les sols du site.". Click "Précédent": "Non" is selected, no surface input.
3. Pick "Oui", enter `60` (% mode), click "Valider".
4. Observe: the heading is "Nous allons maintenant parler de ce que seront les sols du site.". Click "Précédent": "Oui" is selected and "Part à dépolluer" shows `60` with "%" checked and "💡 Soit 1 200 m²".
5. Click "㎡". Observe: the input shows `1200`. Click "Précédent" then "Suivant" to come back to the merged step. Observe: "Oui" selected, "㎡" checked, input `1200`.
6. Click "%" to restore percentage mode.

## S5 — "Ne sait pas" generates a decontamination expense from 25 % of the contaminated soils (regression)

1. Reach the merged step (fresh creation). Pick "Ne sait pas", click "Valider".
2. Continue the creation: soils transformation introduction "Suivant"; "Que souhaitez-vous faire des sols du site ?": "Conserver les sols en l'état", "Valider"; soils summary "Suivant"; carbon storage "Suivant"; stakeholders introduction "Suivant"; developer, operator and reinstatement contract owner: pick any structure; site purchase: "Non / Ne sait pas"; expenses introduction "Suivant".
3. Observe: the heading is "Dépenses de travaux de remise en état de la friche"; the "Dépollution des sols" amount is `33 000` (500 m² × 66 €/m²), **not** `0`.

## S6 — Reinstatement expenses are recomputed only when the resolved surface changes

1. On the reinstatement expenses page (continuing S5), click "Valider" without editing.
2. Click "Précédent" until the heading is "Est-il nécessaire de dépolluer les sols ?" (the creation stepper has no sub-items to jump there). Pick "Oui", enter `60` (% mode, 1 200 m²), click "Valider".
3. Observe: a dialog "La modification de cette étape entraîne d'autres modifications." lists, under "Bénéfriches recalculera automatiquement les montants suivants :", the expenses step ("Dépenses et recettes"). Click "Valider" in the dialog.
4. Click "Suivant" / "Valider" on each page (answers are kept) until the reinstatement expenses page. Observe: "Dépollution des sols" is now `79 200` (1 200 m² × 66).
5. "Précédent" back to the merged step: keep "Oui" with `60` % (the same resolved surface), click "Valider". Observe: **no** dialog appears; the soils transformation introduction is shown directly. Move forward to the reinstatement expenses page: "Dépollution des sols" is still `79 200`.
6. Back to the merged step: pick "Ne sait pas", click "Valider". Observe: the dialog appears again; confirm. Move forward to the reinstatement expenses page: "Dépollution des sols" is `33 000`.
7. Back to the merged step: pick "Oui", enter `25` % (500 m², the same resolved surface as "Ne sait pas"), click "Valider". Observe: **no** dialog.

## S7 — Update pre-fills the merged step from the saved surface

1. Open `http://localhost:3001/mes-projets/<PV_PARTIAL>/modifier`.
2. Observe: the "Récapitulatif du projet" is shown; its "Transformation des sols" section reads "Surface dépolluée: 1 200 m²". No "Choix de dépolluer" / "Surface à dépolluer" anywhere.
3. Click "Travaux" → "Dépollution des sols". Observe: under "Travaux" the stepper lists "Remise en état", then "Dépollution des sols" (current), then the soils items ("Sols du projet"…); heading "Est-il nécessaire de dépolluer les sols ?", "Oui" selected, "Part à dépolluer" = `60` in % mode (or `1200` in ㎡ mode) and "💡 Soit 1 200 m²" (or "Soit 60%"). The query string contains `etape=depollution-sols`.
4. Open `http://localhost:3001/mes-projets/<PV_UNKNOWN>/modifier`, then "Travaux" → "Dépollution des sols". Observe: "Ne sait pas" selected, no surface input.
5. Open `http://localhost:3001/mes-projets/<PV_NONE>/modifier`, then "Travaux" → "Dépollution des sols". Observe: "Non" selected, no surface input. The summary shows no "Surface dépolluée" line.

## S8 — Update: change the answer and save

1. Open `http://localhost:3001/mes-projets/<PV_PARTIAL>/modifier`, then "Travaux" → "Dépollution des sols".
2. Observe: "Oui" selected with `60` %. Replace the value with `30`, click "Valider" (confirm the dialog if one appears).
3. Observe: back on the "Récapitulatif du projet"; "Surface dépolluée" reads "600 m²".
4. Click "Valider" at the bottom of the summary. Observe: "Modifications sauvegardées".
5. Reload `http://localhost:3001/mes-projets/<PV_PARTIAL>/modifier`. Observe: "Surface dépolluée" 600 m²; the merged step shows "Oui" with `30` %.
